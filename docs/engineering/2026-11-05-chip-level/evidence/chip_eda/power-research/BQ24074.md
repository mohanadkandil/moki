## Recommended starting configuration, still requiring sourced passives and review

For an explicitly qualified external5V **1.5A-capable** source, EN2=high and EN1=low select the ILIM-resistor mode. CE#=low enables charging. USB100 is EN2=0/EN1=0; USB500 is0/1;1/1 is suspend. Neither a USB-C receptacle nor5.1k CC pull-downs alone establish that1.5A is available. The plan must state its qualified source/adapter/current-detection condition. [TI p.9 table7-2](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24074.pdf#page=9)

Candidate resistor values, calculated from **current electrical-table bounds**, not newly measured:

- **ILIM1.2kΩ,1%**: typical current limit1610/1200≈1.342A; worst high1720/(1200×0.99)≈**1.448A**. This stays below a1.5A source limit before other source-side loads. Guaranteed-low factor1500 and+1% resistor imply about1.238A lower-limit capability. System+charge budgets must use the guaranteed available limit, not only the nominal1.5A label.
- **ISET3.0kΩ,1%**: typical890/3000≈**297mA** charge; high975/(3000×0.99)≈**328mA**; low797/(3000×1.01)≈263mA. Treat330mA as a screening upper bound. The actual cell must permit that charge rate; no capacity or chemistry was inferred from a PH connector.
- **ITERM open**: manufacturer default10% of programmed charge current in this mode; do not ground pin15 as though it were another variant's TD pin.
- **TMR open**: default precharge1800s and fast-charge18000s nominal (30min/5h), with table tolerances; thermal/DPPM conditions affect timing. The standalone Figure10-12 groundsTMR and therefore disables safety timers: do not copy that choice silently.
- **TS10kNTC**: use a battery-coupled10kNTC consistent with the103AT-2 reference and its required temperature range. A10k fixed resistor is permitted by TI to maintain charging but **disables battery-temperature monitoring**. A two-contact JST-PH battery lead supplies no NTC signal by itself; add/declare the sensor connection or disclose the missing pack-temperature protection.
- Initial capacitance candidates: IN1µF, OUT10µF, BAT4.7µF, ceramic, placed close to the IC and grounded locally. Verify effective capacitance/DC-bias, pulse behavior and actual sourced values; TI permits1–10µFIN and4.7–47µFOUT/BAT. Larger nominal values may be needed for actual pulsed loads.

Primary sources: [TI pp.12–13 electrical bounds](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24074.pdf#page=12), [pp.34–35 programming/component guidance](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24074.pdf#page=34), [p.38 standalone example](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24074.pdf#page=38).

The worked p.34 example uses KILIM1550AΩ, while the current electrical table gives1610typical/1720maximum in this range. Preserve that discrepancy and calculate source protection using the current maximum factor. An1100Ω nominal resistor may allow approximately1.56A even before resistor tolerance and therefore is unsuitable for an unconditional1.5A-input claim.

## SYS to TPS63001 and operating states

BQ24074 separates the system load from the charger/battery node. Connect **OUT10/11→TPS63001VIN**, not BAT2/3→system. This lets dynamic power-path management reduce charging while prioritizing system demand and avoids the simple-MCP73831 termination problem. Tie all relevant grounds and exposed pads through the reviewed local power layout. [TI pp.21–23](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24074.pdf#page=21)

With adequate input headroom, OUT regulation is4.3/4.4/4.5V min/typ/max. Without input, OUT is battery voltage **minus the battery-path drop**: at1A, VBAT>3V, the electrical table gives50mVtypical/100mVmaximum. Thus a3.0V minimum battery does **not** by itself guarantee a3.0V minimum SYS. Input-to-OUT dropout is300mVtypical/475mVmaximum at the stated1A test condition. The exact minimum SYS must include source/cell tolerances, battery protection cutoff, load and wiring drops. A broad SYS range passed to TPS63001 can be modeled only after choosing those assumptions. [TI p.12](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24074.pdf#page=12)

TPS63001's1.8–5.5V input range covers nominal battery/SYS operation, but its3.3V output-current and efficiency envelope remains input-dependent. A3.3V500mA load at3.0VSYS and assumed90% efficiency draws approximately611mA before charger and otherSYSloads. At2.7V it draws≈679mA. These are estimates, not performance measurements; audio peaks and startup need their own budget.

The current generic ERC dependency contract describes one declared source state. At minimum check separately: USBpresent+battery charging+system running; USBabsent+battery supplyingSYS; current-limited USB with supplement/DPPM behavior; battery absent. A USB-only passing graph does not prove battery fallback, nor does a battery-only source declare charging availability. Do not connect an external battery-source declaration in parallel with the BATcharger source and waive a multiple-source error without a reviewed state model.

## Shared thermal constraint

The chip dissipates losses from **both** its SYS path and charging path. TI's equation is:

`P = (VIN − VOUT) × (IOUT + IBAT) + (VOUT − VBAT) × IBAT`

Equivalent: `(VIN−VOUT)×IOUT + (VIN−VBAT)×IBAT`. Sum losses for the same physical IC before applying a junction-temperature limit; separate per-output checks can miss overheating. The thermal table quotes θJA44.5°C/W under its test conditions. Thermal regulation begins near125°C; shutdown near155°C. These are not a license to design typical operation at the protection threshold. The exposed pad must be soldered toVSS thermal copper with appropriate vias. Actual PCB/enclosure thermal behavior remains unmeasured. [TI pp.10–11,13,28–29,42](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24074.pdf#page=42)

Calculated nominal example: VIN5V, SYS4.4V, cell3.0V, system current0.6A, charge0.3A →0.36W+0.60W≈0.96W. At θJA44.5°C/W this predicts≈43°C rise. At ambient25°C that is≈68°C; at85°C it is≈128°C and enters thermal regulation. With5.25V input and system tolerance/battery assumptions the result can worsen. These numbers are screening estimates only; use actual summed loads, capacitor behavior and copper-dependent thermal data.

BQ24074 supports battery temperature sensing and safety timers but does not replace the battery pack's overdischarge/short-circuit protection. The PH connector's2A rating at the stated wire condition does not establish cell charge/discharge capability. Open-drainPGOOD#/CHG# may be pulled to3.3V for MCU monitoring; this differs from MCP73831's actively driven-highSTAT.
