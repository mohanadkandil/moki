## Findings and correction status

Three confirmed medium-severity issues were sent to the implementer immediately. All three corrections are present in the source at this review's final read. Regenerated artifacts and acceptance tests remain the coordinator's verification responsibility.

1. **Rounded PCB outline lost at the mechanical handoff** — `eda/enclosure/handoff.ts:170` previously always emitted four bounding-rectangle corners, even when `pcb_board.outline` contained the new 52-point fillet. Consequently `mech/enclosure.py:156` could never recognize the rounded generated board and used the legacy radius. **Corrected:** handoff now copies finite outline coordinates, retaining the rectangular fallback for absent outlines. This dependency was read outside the assigned edit scope; the reviewer did not edit it.
2. **Assembly deleted the mask-material contract** — `eda/enclosure/assembly.ts:159` previously replaced `asset.extras.moki` with a legacy provenance string. This removed `maskMaterials`, `modelVersion`, and `defaultMask`, making the documented mask picker contract unavailable in case/exploded views. Confirmed in the first generated focus-timer `assembly.glb`. **Corrected:** source preserves `asset.extras` and stores the provenance separately.
3. **LED leads missed the catalog pads by 1 mm in Y** — `eda/models/catalog-models.ts:141` previously inverted the LED's Y offset before the common catalog-to-circuit transform inverted it again. The asymmetric A/K positions became A=-6 and K=-7 instead of A=-7 and K=-6 in footprint coordinates. Overall envelope tests did not detect this. **Corrected:** source uses catalog-down local offsets and lets the final transform invert once; the implementer is adding a pad-endpoint regression.

No further confirmed consequential defects found in the remaining reviewed scope.

### Follow-up: lossless GLB compaction

No blocking defect found for this renderer's current static GLB output. Vertex sharing uses the complete attribute tuple within each primitive, so differing normals and UVs retain separate vertices and materials remain separated by primitive. The original index sequence is remapped rather than reordered.
