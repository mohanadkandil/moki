### Open confirmed findings

**P1 — promotion accepts missing electrical and block-test gates** — `src/blocks/library.ts:30`.

`recordIndependentReview` requires a nonempty all-`ok` gate list with any DRC gate. Thus a test receipt containing only `{gate: 'drc', ok: true, issues: []}` passes its gate predicate and can promote an exact-hash independent passing review without ERC, power or block-test evidence. It also trusts `ok` even when `issues` contains an error. This defeats the stated promotion contract even though the operation correctly remains internal and is not exported through the author-facing barrel. Require the complete relevant gate set, reject missing/duplicate gates and error issues, and exercise DRC-only and contradictory-`ok` receipts in isolated library tests. Compile/SPICE applicability should be explicit rather than silently inferred from an omitted gate.

**P1 — MCU pin capabilities never constrain the connected function** — `src/blocks/erc.ts:115`, `src/blocks/erc.ts:362–427`.

`McuFacts.pins[].caps` is accepted by the schema but never consumed by ERC or pin-map generation. In the existing synthetic STM32 setup, changing PA0/PA1 capabilities from `['input', 'output', 'i2c']` to `['input']` leaves graph validation and generated I2C SDA/SCL pin mappings unchanged. This is established by complete source inspection and absence of any `caps` consumer, not a reviewer-run test. An input-only or function-incompatible GPIO therefore passes the promised pin-function-conflict gate. Validate actual connected signal requirements against capabilities and reject unsupported or reserved GPIO assignments during ERC; keep deliberately optional/unconnected MCU pins legal. Add a regression that changes only the MCU capability facts while retaining the same links.

**P2 — review status uses lexical timestamp order rather than chronology** — `src/blocks/library.ts:21`.

The internal review operation accepts any parseable date. `trusted()` sorts those dates as strings, whereas ERC's `review()` sorts epoch timestamps. A pass at `2026-11-05T12:00:00Z` plus a later fail at `2026-11-05T11:30:00-02:00` (13:30 UTC) makes the library report `reviewed` from the older pass; ERC correctly chooses the later fail. This can mislabel a block in library/UI/BOM metadata and makes approval state inconsistent across interfaces. Compare parsed timestamps consistently (or normalize valid timestamps to UTC on recording), and cover a newer failure expressed with a timezone offset.
