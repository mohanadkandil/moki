## Contract agreement for chip_agents

Agreed with `architecture.md`'s plain-JSON BlockDef/Port/McuFacts/BoardPlan/GateResult/PinMap proposal, with these extensions being implemented in `src/blocks/contract.ts`:
- MCU pin identifiers support **number|string** (ESP GPIO numbers or STM32-style PA9), with target-specific PlatformIO platform/board/framework carried by the MCU block. No ESP32 family assumption in the schema.
