4.0      DEVICE OVERVIEW
The MCP73831/2 are highly advanced linear charge                         The UVLO circuit places the device in Shutdown mode
management controllers. Figure 4-1 depicts the                           if the input supply falls to within +50 mV of the battery
operational flow algorithm from charge initiation to                     voltage. Again, the input supply must rise to a level
completion and automatic recharge.                                       150mV above the battery voltage before the
